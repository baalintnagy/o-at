O-NEC

Open Noise and Echo Cancelation · VST3, CLAP, standalone

What voice chat applications do to your microphone, as a plugin you can put on any microphone chain, with adjustable parameters and detailed visual feedback.

  • Noise suppression with a choice of two neural models: RNNoise for steady noise (32 ms total latency) or DeepFilterNet3, which also removes keyboards and other non-stationary noise (72 ms).
  • Echo cancellation learns the loudspeaker-to-microphone path from the Echo Reference sidechain, plus a residual echo suppressor.
  • Voice gate that opens on detected speech or above a level.
  • Speech leveler that raises whispering and lowers shouting, measured only while someone speaks.
  • Listen removed solos what the chain takes away, to tune settings without damaging speech.

O-NR

Open Noise Reduction · VST3, CLAP, standalone

Removes noise from any material: music, field recordings, film dialogue and voice. It learns what the noise sounds like from a passage where only the noise is heard, then removes it wherever it lies under the signal.

  • Press LEARN during a noise-only passage; the profile is stored with the session.
  • Without a profile, or in Adaptive mode, it tracks steady noise under the signal by itself.
  • Reduction, Threshold and Resolution: higher resolutions separate hum and other steady tones better, at more delay, which hosts compensate.
  • Listen removed plays what is taken away.

O-AUD-TX and O-AUD-RX

Audio routing · VST3, CLAP, applications

Route audio between hosts, applications and devices through named buses, for example from a DAW to a voice chat application or to headphones on another interface. Buses live in shared memory and need no server or virtual driver.

  • O-AUD-TX sends its track to a bus with a name you choose; its own output passes through or is muted.
  • O-AUD-RX plays a bus inside any host, or on any output device with the standalone application, resampling to follow the sender's clock.
  • The O-AUD-TX application sends any number of routes: an input device, everything an output device plays, or what a single application plays (the last two on Windows).
  • Any number of receivers can play the same bus; O-AUD-TX shows its listeners.

O-AUD-A

Audio analyzer · VST3, CLAP, standalone

Insert it anywhere; it passes the audio through unchanged. Its pages show a spectrum analyzer, a sonogram, a stereo imager, an oscilloscope, an EBU R 128 loudness meter and 3D spectrum and stereo views, side by side on a resizable overview.

  • Sources: compare buses from O-AUD-TX with the input, each in its own color, with level matching and masking.
  • Platforms shows what streaming services' loudness normalization does to the track.
  • Full screen, free resizing, and per-view settings and colors.

Install

Windows x64 · Linux x64

Windows

Download

Get O-AT-X.Y.Z-windows-x64.zip from the latest release and unpack it.

Copy the plugins

Copy the folders in VST3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and the files in CLAP to …\Common Files\CLAP (administrator rights needed).

Rescan

Rescan plugins in your host; they are listed under the vendor o-at. The applications run from any folder.

Linux

Download

Get O-AT-X.Y.Z-linux-x64.tar.gz from the latest release and unpack it. It needs glibc 2.35 or later (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12) and X11 or XWayland.

Copy the plugins

Into your home folder, no root needed:

mkdir -p ~/.vst3 ~/.clap
cp -r VST3/*.vst3 ~/.vst3/
cp CLAP/*.clap ~/.clap/

Rescan

Rescan plugins in your host. The applications run from the unpacked folder; O-AUD-TX sends input devices on Linux.

Details in INSTALL.txt (Windows) and INSTALL-linux.txt. To build from source, see the README.

O-AT is built with Rust on NIH-plug and egui, with nnnoiseless and DeepFilterNet. Its audio paths are tested for allocations and, on Linux, with RealtimeSanitizer.
